Clojure Sequence Learning Plan
==============================

Goal: Master Clojure sequences through illustrative examples.

Phase 1: The Sequence Abstraction [DONE]
---------------------------------
Concepts: ISeq interface, first, rest, cons, seq.
Goal: Understand that sequences are a logical view, not a physical collection.
Planned Example: `src/basics.clj`
Verified: `test/seq_learn/basics_test.clj`

Phase 2: Sequence Generation [DONE]
----------------------------
Concepts: range, repeat, replicate, cycle, iterate.
Goal: Learn how to create sequences programmatically.
Planned Example: `src/generation.clj`

Phase 3: Transformations [DONE]
------------------------
Concepts: map, filter, remove, mapcat, keep.
Goal: Master core sequence transformation functions.
Planned Example: `src/transformations.clj`

Phase 4: Subsequences & Partitioning [DONE]
------------------------------------
Concepts: take, drop, split-at, take-while, drop-while, partition, partition-by.
Goal: Learn how to slice and dice sequences.
Planned Example: `src/subsequences.clj`

Phase 5: Laziness & Infinite Sequences
--------------------------------------
Concepts: LazySeq, realizing sequences, infinite generators.
Goal: Understand the power and pitfalls of laziness.
Planned Example: `src/laziness.clj`

Phase 6: Performance & Chunking
-------------------------------
Concepts: Chunked sequences, transducers (intro).
Goal: Learn how Clojure optimizes sequence processing.
Planned Example: `src/performance.clj`
